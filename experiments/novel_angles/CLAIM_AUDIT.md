# Claim-by-claim EEG re-verification audit

**Scoring source:** PR HEAD `bf306cde2b354211c57d770b2433c41dcc0b36cd`, before documentation edits. This is a repeat calculation on the same data, not a new sample or an independent replication. Raw data, per-person FIFs, and private split/provenance logs are not committed. Fresh filenames below are relative to the private run directory `/home/ubuntu/eeg-pr-reverification/` on the scoring machine; committed aggregate counterparts are linked where available.

**How to read this audit:** *Fresh check* means the calculation or direct comparison was rerun. *Cached preparation* means the prepared files and recorded preparation evidence were reused; it does not mean another full raw-data preprocessing run. *Source check* means protocol, source code, or a publication was inspected rather than an empirical calculation. *Historical* means the statement was preserved from an earlier run, not rerun here. Values used in prose are rounded from the underlying aggregates, not subtracted after rounding. Nothing was tuned to produce a match.

**Fusion tie correction:** The original Round 6 implementation (`293699d`) divided each empirical rank by its calibration count before averaging. Separately rounded percentiles could split equal rank sums. `round6.py:fused_calibration_score` sums integer ranks first and divides once by twice the common calibration count. The four reruns recompute the fused cutoffs and metrics; only the `fusion` objects changed. WBCIC-SHU went from 37 to 25 distinct fused scores. Dortmund's unadjusted fusion-minus-cosine FAR interval moved from [−0.5035, +0.0052] to [−0.5051, −0.0008] pp, narrowly excluding zero after the correction; the fused FAR still exceeds 1%.
