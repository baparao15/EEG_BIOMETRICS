# Round 4 candidate screen

This targeted search checks close precedents, not universal priority. The ranking reflects both plausible novelty and value as the next *journal* experiment; it is not a score forecast. “Not tested” means no new result. Use the evidence and novelty scales in [REPORT.md](REPORT.md). This screen reads publisher or repository abstracts and, where indicated, available methods; it is not a systematic review.

| 1 | **Distinct-identity risk control with an abstention option.** Can we say when independent impostor coverage is insufficient for the stated FAR target? | [Conformal Risk Control](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2208.02814) controls expected monotone loss under exchangeability; a case-specific EEG procedure must handle claimant–impostor dependence and visit shift. Unlike the earlier identity-vs-time diagnosis, this would prescribe when not to make a FAR claim. Not tested. | 3 | 1 |
| 2 | **Identity-maximum calibration.** Do per-impostor maxima improve frozen attempt-level FAR versus pooled windows? | [Joint user-specific and sample bootstraps](https://doi.org/10.1109/tpami.2007.55) explicitly account for correlations in biometric scores for uncertainty estimation. [Su et al.](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-73033-7_14) use maximum scores over gallery identities for open-set identification (methods inspected); here the maximum is over repeated attempts by each calibration impostor against one claimant. Tested; this is not a risk-control guarantee. | 3 | 4 |
| 3 | **Transport a frozen cutoff across datasets.** Train templates locally, but carry over a decision cutoff from another dataset without target-domain tuning. | [Data-specific adaptive thresholds](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.1810.11160) adapts thresholds for face recognition; here the deliberate *absence* of target recalibration exposes cutoff portability. The previous EEG replications froze cutoffs within each dataset, not across datasets. Not tested. | 3 | 1 |
| 4 | **Worst-state agreement at a fixed duration.** Require both state-specific scores to clear the same cutoff instead of averaging features. | [Kittler et al.](https://doi.org/10.1109/34.667881) describe the minimum rule and compare fusion for face/voice verification (methods inspected); the new endpoint is a within-attempt CE/OE agreement ablation under matched time and a frozen FAR cutoff. Tested. | 2 | 2 |
| 5 | **Enrollment repeatability as feature weighting.** Weight spectral coordinates, not users, by their enrollment between-person versus within-person variance. | [Feature selection for nonstationary data](https://doi.org/10.1109/tcyb.2017.2702059) selects persistent cross-session ECG/TEOAE features with an auxiliary dataset. Here weighting is soft, state-stratified, and fitted on independent reference EEG enrollment windows. Tested. | 2 | 2 |
| 6 | **Reactivity consistency as a penalty on paired matching.** Penalize a paired cosine score if the posterior alpha change differs from the claimant's enrollment change. | [Choi et al. 2018](https://doi.org/10.9718/JBER.2018.39.1.43) already compare spatial patterns of EEG alpha reactivity for authentication; [Choi et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1109/IWW-BCI.2019.8737254) compare a standalone CE–OE PSD representation. The narrow extension is this scalar penalty and its frozen FAR/FRR trade-off, not discovery of identity information in reactivity. Tested. | 2 | 4 |
| 7 | **Temporal agreement under a transaction budget.** Compare a fixed sequence of paired attempts with equal-time pooling; make retry and abstention explicit in the endpoint. | [Allano et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.patrec.2010.01.028) use sequential probability-ratio fusion to tune biometric cost and performance across matchers. The extension would be within-person EEG attempt dependence and fixed total acquisition time, a different transaction endpoint from Loop 2. Not tested. | 2 | 1 |
| 8 | **Per-window quality weighting.** Weight the two eye states by prespecified acquisition-quality indicators from the retained windows, rather than by spectral reliability learned on other people. | [BioSecure DS2](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIFS.2009.2034885) benchmarks quality-dependent face, fingerprint and iris score fusion. The proposed endpoint is paired resting EEG at unchanged duration; quality must not encode identity or leak test outcomes. Not tested. | 2 | 1 |
| 9 | **Wrong-person competition.** Test a claimant score's margin over the strongest independent reference profile instead of mean/variance score normalization. | [Rosenberg et al.](https://doi.org/10.21437/icslp.1992-176) and [Reynolds](https://doi.org/10.21437/eurospeech.1997-337) compare claimant scores with cohort/background models in speaker verification. The incremental endpoint is maximum competing-profile margin with separate reference identities and frozen 1:1 thresholds. Not tested. | 2 | 1 |
| 10 | **Repeatability-aware left/right contrasts.** Compare relative homologous-channel features against absolute features at the same probe budget. | [Interhemispheric amplitude ratio for EEG person identification](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0238872) already uses left/right EEG ratios. The narrow extension is measuring their day-to-day stability and frozen FAR in paired probes, not introducing asymmetry features. Not tested. | 1 | 1 |
